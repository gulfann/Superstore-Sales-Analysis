# Superstore-Sales-Analysis

# 📊 Retail Profitability & Customer Analytics

### Data-driven analysis of sales, profitability, discounting, and customer value

> **Where is the company's profit coming from, where is it being lost, and how can data guide smarter business decisions?**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

A national retail chain has experienced steady revenue growth from **2014–2017**, but profit growth has not kept pace. Some products and states generate strong sales while producing little or negative profit.

This project analyzes four years of order-level transaction data to identify **profitability drivers, discounting impact, customer value, regional performance, and seasonal trends**.

The goal is to turn raw transactional data into actionable recommendations for **pricing, regional strategy, product management, and customer retention**.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions

* Which product sub-categories and states are profitable or consistently loss-making?
* Does discounting improve sales enough to justify reduced profit?
* At what discount level does profitability begin to decline?
* How do sales and profit change across months, seasons, and years?
* Which customers are most valuable?
* Which valuable customers may be at risk of churn?
* How does profitability vary by region and customer segment?
